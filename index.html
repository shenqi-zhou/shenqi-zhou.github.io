Auto-MD-bench: Evaluating Autoresearch Agents for Real-World Medical Tasks

Shenqi Zhou*, Qiran Zou*, Wenhao Zhao, Zhenyu Liao, Dianbo Liu

arXiv preprint, 2026. Under review. *Equal contribution.

arXiv · PDF · Code · Data

We find that the strongest agents score higher than simple baseline models on the main metric, but their models’ risk estimates are less well calibrated than those of the baselines. The models’ performance also varies across patient populations.

Research

Auto-MD-bench

Aug 2026 – present

Research intern at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Dianbo Liu

I led an independent research project investigating whether optimizing a single performance metric is sufficient to guide autoresearch agents toward models for real-world medical use. I designed and built Auto-MD-bench, which covers survival analysis, classification and sequential prediction tasks, with external validation across patient populations. I also evaluated the calibration of the agents’ models and the modeling choices the agents made.

Evaluating Chinese sentence understanding in large language models

Apr 2025 – Mar 2026

Undergraduate researcher at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Nai Ding

I led a team of three students investigating how well LLMs understand Chinese sentences and how their sentence spaces compare with those derived from human judgments. We collected entailment judgments from people and LLMs on a set of Chinese sentences we created. The LLMs agreed closely with people on semantic relatedness but much less on entailment direction.

Skin lesion classification on HAM10000

Spring 2026

Biomedical Image Processing course, Zhejiang University · Team lead (five students)

Our team built classifiers for seven types of skin lesions in dermoscopy images. I reviewed everyone’s work and combined it into the final report. My own part was the evaluation and error analysis, for example looking at melanomas that the models misclassified as benign nevi.
