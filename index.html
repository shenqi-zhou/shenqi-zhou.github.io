Hi, I’m Shenqi. I’m a final-year undergraduate in Biomedical Engineering at Zhejiang University. Since May 2026, I’ve also been a research intern at the National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Dianbo Liu. I’m interested in AI for healthcare and in how we evaluate LLMs and AI agents.

My current project, Auto-MD-bench, evaluates AI agents that build medical prediction models on their own, looking beyond a single performance score. Before that, I worked with Prof. Nai Ding at Zhejiang University, comparing how people and LLMs understand Chinese sentences.

Manuscript

Auto-MD-bench: Evaluating Autoresearch Agents for Real-World Medical Tasks

Shenqi Zhou*, Qiran Zou*, Wenhao Zhao, Zhenyu Liao, Dianbo Liu

arXiv preprint, 2026. Under review. *Equal contribution.

arXiv · PDF · Code · Data

We find that models built by AI agents can reach competitive primary scores while giving risk estimates that are less well calibrated than those of simple baseline models, so a single score is not enough to judge them for medical use.

Evaluating Chinese sentence understanding in large language models

Apr 2025 – Mar 2026

Undergraduate researcher at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Nai Ding

I compared how people and three LLMs judge Chinese sentence pairs: how related they are in meaning, and in which direction one implies the other. I built the sentence set, collected human judgments and adapted Sentence-Space Metrics for the comparison. The models agreed with people more on relatedness than on entailment direction.
